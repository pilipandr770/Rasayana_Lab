Spermidine already holds EU Novel Food status (Regulation 2020/443); Amalaki/gallic acid has traditional food-use status. The most expensive and longest budget line (the Novel Food dossier, €300–500k, 12–24 months) applies only to cycloastragenol. This opens a capital-efficient path: launch a pilot batch on the two already-cleared components now, and add cycloastragenol as a second product wave once the dossier is funded by a separate tranche or partner. Spermidin hat bereits den EU-Novel-Food-Status (Verordnung 2020/443); Amalaki/Gallussäure hat den Status einer traditionellen Lebensmittelverwendung.
